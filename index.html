Smell the fantastic aroma of fresh baked goods in Accra?

Chances are you're passing by our bakery or receiving a fresh batch. Stop in or order fresh bakes for your breakfast, lunch, or family tea. We bake fresh products every single morning.

Biggabs Bakery began in 2019 in Abigail Amoah's kitchen in Accra. Rooted in cherished Ghanaian baking traditions, Abigail elevates beloved local favorites with pure butter, real nutmeg, and zero commercial compromises.
